    What this tool does and does not do

    It records and prints the Schedule H1 sale register. It does not transmit anything to any authority, has no connection to any drug-control portal, and is not a substitute for the bound physical register where one is required: a bound, serially paginated register is what an inspector asks to see, and many state drug-control authorities expect it. Use this to keep the data straight, to find an entry quickly and to print clean pages — not to replace the book.

    The register holds patient names and addresses. The data file is created readable only by your user account; back it up the way you would back up any patient record, and keep it for at least the retention period.

    Figures this tool uses

    Retention period: 3 years from the date of supply, under the Schedule H1 condition read with Rule 65(11A) of the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945. Three calendar years, not 1,095 days. A supply on 29 February is retained until 28 February three years later; hold it to 1 March if you would rather keep a day in hand.

    Column set: the Schedule H1 condition itself names the prescriber, the patient, the drug and the quantity. The batch number, manufacturer, prescriber's registration number and dispensing pharmacist are included because inspection formats in practice ask for them. All eleven are required here.

    Confirm the current Schedule H1 list and the register format with your State Drugs Controller or on the CDSCO website before relying on either.
